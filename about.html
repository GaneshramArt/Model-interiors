About the studio

We design spaces around the people who inhabit them.

A small team of interior architects, designers and site managers working across South India.

The studio

Architecture, design and emotion, brought together to create spaces that inspire.

Model Interiors designs homes around the people who live in them.

Every detail is considered, from spatial proportions and material transitions to lighting, joinery and the objects that complete the room. We work across villas, apartments and private residences, from the first plan to the final cushion.

Our palette is quiet on purpose: natural stone, warm timber, brushed brass and soft textiles, arranged so that light and proportion do most of the work.

People & studio

We design spaces around the people who inhabit them.

We are a small team of interior architects, designers and site managers. We keep the team small so the people who draw your home are the same people who stand in it during installation.

Most of our work happens at a table covered in samples, or on site with a tape measure. Very little of it happens in presentations.
