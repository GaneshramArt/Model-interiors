What a complete project includes

Every turnkey commission follows the same scope, so you always know what is covered.

Design

  • Measured survey and brief
  • Space planning and layouts
  • Material and finish palette
  • 3D visualisation of key rooms

Documentation

Execution

  • Bespoke joinery and installation
